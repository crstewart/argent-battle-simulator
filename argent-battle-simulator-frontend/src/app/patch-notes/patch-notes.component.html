Version 1.2

  • Myths and Monsters

    We all have our monsters that we face. Whether we fight external monsters, our own inner demons, or we are our own monsters is an entirely different story. The new entrants to the battlefield all deal with their own monstrosities. But there is help: The gods and goddesses of the multiverse are making their presence known as well. The question this time is, "Will you put your faith in the divine or succumb to your own personal monsters?"
  • New Mechanic: Status Effects
    • Some attacks and traits apply status effects to the opponent.
    • Poison: Deals toxic damage at the end of every turn; mech characters are immune
    • Bleed: Deals slash damage at the end of every turn; mech characters are immune
    • Burn: Deals fire damage at the end of every turn; characters with the trait Heat Resistance are immune
    • Paralysis: Halves the speed stat; characters with the trait Natural Agility are immune
    • Sleep: Prevents the character from acting until a certain number of turns pass or they are hit by an attack; characters with the trait Insomniac are immune
    • Root: Prevents the character from moving or using melee attacks; characters with the trait Teleportation are immune
    • Slow: Reduces the priority of all moves by 1; characters with the trait Time Manipulation are immune
    • Silence: Prevents the character from using magic attacks
    • Frostbite: Halves the agility stat; characters with the trait Cold Resistance are immune
  • New Mechanic: Homing and Hold
    • Homing and Hold attack characteristics will replace most Undodgeable and Unguardable types.
    • Characters with the new trait Time Manipulation can dodge homing attacks.
    • Characters with the new trait Teleportation can guard hold attacks.
    • X-Strikes will continue to be Undodgeable and Unguardable regardless of traits!

  • Reign of Shadows

    Villains, bad guys, anti-heroes. Whatever label you give them, they've arrived en masse onto the battlefield. Our battle-tested heroes will have their hands full with these new enemies. Of course, some of these villains will meet resistance from familiar foes of their own. But that's not all! Some new heroes are rising from the shadows themselves and sneaking their way into the fray as well!
  • New Mechanic: Stealth
    • Stealth attacks act as an attack and a dodge rolled up into one move. The hit chance for the enemy's attack is reduced, boosted by the attacker's speed, agility, and small size. In addition to the increased dodge chance, the attack cannot be guarded or dodged. However, these attacks are usually weak in power.
    • The increased dodge chance on a stealth attack can be negated by a character that has the new trait Stealth Detection.
