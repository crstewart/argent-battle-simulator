Would you like to contribute to this project?

I'm looking for help with the Argent Battle Simulator! If you would like to help out in any of these categories, then please reach out to me at contribute [[at]] argentbattlesimulator.com. You could be credited and/or compensated for your contributions:

  • Character Suggestions: The simulator is not nearly all-encompassing. There are many more characters than can or even should be added! Some characters might need reworks as well! The only rule I am enforcing for the simulator is that a character must have originated in or be most well-known for being from a video game. Characters from TV, movies, anime, and so on will be rejected. Character rework suggestions are welcome additions as well!
  • Security Testers: The simulator needs someone who can help me keep up with the security of the application for current and future functionality. Experience is required!
  • Application Testers: The simulator lacks sufficient code coverage currently. If you are good with writing Junit 5 unit tests, writing integration tests, or are looking to pick up these skills then I can provide codebase access. If you want to black box test the website, then that help is appreciated as well!
  • Functional Coders: Many new features are planned for the simulator such as duels, tournaments, customizable fantasy seasons, and custom-built characters! If you have experience with or are interested in learning Java, Javascript, Typescript, or Angular, then I can grant you access to the codebase to help with adding new functionality!
  • Discord Integration: I am looking for someone to help with creating a Discord bot! If you have the experience or are seeking it, then I can work with you on that!
  • AI Development: The simulator has very barebones intelligence currently. I would like to expand upon that in the future. If you have experience with developing AI for games, then let me know!
  • Artists: I would like to include custom art for the simulator. As it stands, all the art is either public domain or official character art sited from the copyright owner. (If your fan art is featured by mistake, let me know and I will work with you to correct this!) If you want to contribute character art, then I can work with you on that!
